### CHEM-000007: Carbon isotope exchange at arbitrary skeletal positions
- **Subfield:** Organic synthesis and medicinal chemistry
- **Open:** Exchange a chosen skeletal carbon for an isotope-labeled carbon in an intact complex molecule without reconstructing its skeleton, losing stereochemical information or extensively scrambling the label.
- **Status:** Direct exchange has advanced for selected functional groups. Vanadium-mediated exchange of terminal alkenes was demonstrated in 2025; buried saturated carbons and arbitrary position selection remain open.
- **Type:** experimental
- **Why:** Simplifies tracer preparation and quantitative studies of molecular fate.
- **Key works:** Krivovicheva et al. (2025), *Vanadium-Mediated Carbon Isotope Exchange of Terminal Alkenes*, [DOI:10.1021/jacs.5c05880](https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.5c05880).
- **Related:** Medical imaging: rapid site-specific radiotracer synthesis. Systems biology: identifiable metabolic-flux models.
